## R1.3 / R2.1 - clustered re-analysis

Methods. (a) Clip-level aggregation: the two seeds of each clip are averaged, giving 12 clip-level observations; the quantized-vs-seed contrasts are then clip-matched and tested with the Wilcoxon signed-rank test (n = 12). (b) Permutation: for quantized-vs-seed contrasts, the arm label is permuted within each clip among its three values (two quantized deltas, one seed delta), statistic = difference of medians; for paired contrasts, the sign of the difference is flipped per clip (both seeds together), exact enumeration of 2^12 assignments, statistic = median difference. (c) Linear mixed model with a clip random intercept (statsmodels MixedLM, REML), on the log scale for positive quantities (log ratio for paired contrasts) and on the raw scale otherwise. For Spearman: cluster bootstrap of rho over clips, within-clip permutation of the kinematic delta, Spearman on clip means (n = 12), and a mixed model on standardised ranks.

## R1.4 / R2.1 - equivalence

Margin rule (proposed): the equivalence margin of each statistic is 0.5 x the median BF16 seed-to-seed delta of that statistic over the 12 clips. Justification: the seed-to-seed delta is the change produced by the unquantized model when only the sampling noise changes; a quantization effect smaller than half of the typical sampling effect is not of practical consequence for a per-video acceptance decision, whereas a difference of the order of the sampling effect itself would be. The margin is fixed from the BF16 data before any quantized data enter the contrast. Decision: equivalence is declared when the 90% interval of the contrast lies inside +/- margin (equivalent to two one-sided tests at alpha = 0.05). Two contrasts are reported: the difference of medians with a hierarchical bootstrap interval, and the clip-level mean difference with a paired t-TOST (n = 12). `Margin needed` is the half-width the margin would need for the 90% bootstrap interval to fit, as a multiple of the median seed delta. Non-inferiority (R1.4 alternative): the quantized quantity is not larger than the reference by more than the margin, i.e. the upper limit of the 90% interval is below +margin (one-sided test at alpha = 0.05); larger deltas, larger absolute acceleration variance, jitter, limb-length variability and violation fraction are the unfavourable direction. The Under-motion check (integer count, median seed delta 0) has no defined margin and is not tested.
